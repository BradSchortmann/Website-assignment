Industry of Interest

The industry I am interested in is civil engineering. Civil engineering is a field that focuses on designing, building, and maintaining the infrastructure people depend on every day, such as roads, bridges, drainage systems, buildings, and water systems. This industry is important because it directly affects public safety, transportation, and the quality of life in a community.

Career of Interest

A career that interests me in this industry is transportation engineering. Transportation engineers help design roads, intersections, traffic systems, and transit networks so that people and goods can move safely and efficiently. This career appeals to me because it combines problem-solving, teamwork, and planning with real-world impact on communities.

How Cybersecurity and AI Affect This Career

Cybersecurity and artificial intelligence both play a growing role in civil engineering. Many projects now use digital files, cloud storage, drones, smart sensors, GIS data, and computer-aided design tools. If that information is not protected, it could be stolen, altered, or used in harmful ways. AI also affects civil engineering by helping with traffic modeling, project planning, predictive maintenance, and design analysis. These tools can save time and improve accuracy, but they must be used carefully so that decisions remain safe, ethical, and reliable.
