Top Four Values

1. Safety

Safety is my most important value because civil engineering affects the public every day. Bridges, roads, and buildings must be designed to protect people. This value comes from learning that engineering decisions can have serious real-world consequences.

2. Responsibility

Responsibility matters because engineers must be dependable and careful with their work. This value comes from school, family expectations, and the idea that professionals should be accountable for the quality of their decisions.

3. Honesty

Honesty is important because engineers must report data, problems, and risks truthfully. It comes from personal beliefs that trust is built through integrity and that misleading others can create unsafe results.

4. Respect

Respect matters because civil engineering requires cooperation with clients, coworkers, contractors, and the public. This value comes from experience working with different people and understanding that communication affects project success.

Why These Values Matter

These values shape the kind of engineer I want to become. If I do not follow them, the ramifications could include unsafe designs, damaged trust, project delays, legal trouble, or harm to the public. In civil engineering, ignoring values is not just a personal mistake; it can affect entire communities.
