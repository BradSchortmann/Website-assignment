Ethical Issue 1: Protecting Infrastructure Data

Civil engineering projects often involve sensitive data such as bridge designs, traffic plans, site surveys, and project schedules. If that information is not protected, hackers could steal it or alter it. My personal values of responsibility and honesty would guide me to support strong passwords, secure file sharing, regular updates, and careful access control.

Ethical Issue 2: Using AI Fairly and Safely

AI can help with traffic forecasting, design suggestions, and project planning, but it can also make mistakes or reflect bias in the data. If AI tools are trusted without review, they could lead to unsafe or unfair decisions. My value of safety would shape how I handle this issue, because I would want human professionals to review AI results before making final decisions.

How My Values Shape My Response

My values would guide me to use technology carefully rather than automatically. Respect would remind me to communicate concerns clearly, honesty would push me to report problems instead of hiding them, and responsibility would help me make ethical choices even under pressure. In civil engineering, these values matter because technology should support public safety, not replace careful professional judgment.
