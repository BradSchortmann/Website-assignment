Ethical Issue #1: AI Design Errors

Artificial intelligence is becoming more common in civil engineering for traffic analysis, structural design, and project planning. However, AI systems can make mistakes or provide inaccurate recommendations. If engineers rely too heavily on AI without verification, unsafe designs could be approved and potentially put the public at risk.

How My Values Shape My Response

My values of integrity and responsibility require me to carefully review AI-generated information before making engineering decisions. Even when technology is helpful, engineers must remain accountable for the final outcome.

Ethical Issue #2: Cyberattacks on Infrastructure

Modern transportation systems, traffic signals, and infrastructure networks rely heavily on digital technology. Cyberattacks targeting these systems could disrupt transportation, damage infrastructure, or threaten public safety.

How My Values Shape My Response

My values of safety and responsibility encourage me to support strong cybersecurity practices that protect critical infrastructure and the communities that depend on it.

Conclusion

As AI and technology continue to expand within civil engineering, professionals must balance innovation with ethical responsibility. Strong personal values help ensure technology is used safely and responsibly.
